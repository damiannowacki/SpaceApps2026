🚀 Launch

Our team is taking on the Space Mission Design Game challenge. Pilot a spacecraft through the Solar System and attempt a successful landing on distant astronomical bodies.

Mission Overview

The game challenges players to choose the correct launch time, direction and velocity in order to complete a successful rendezvous with the Moon.

Trajectory calculations take into account the rotation of the Earth, the Moon's orbit around Earth, spacecraft launch velocity and direction, and the effects of gravity throughout the mission.

What Does It Do?

This game simulates spacecraft trajectories during missions from Earth to the Moon, Mars and other destinations throughout the Solar System.

How Does It Work?

Players use SpaceCoin to purchase rockets and configure key mission parameters including:

  • Spacecraft mass
  • Engine thrust
  • Launch direction
  • Mission timing

After configuring the spacecraft, the player presses Launch and observes the simulated flight. Successful missions earn additional SpaceCoin and unlock more challenging destinations.

Educational Benefits

The game introduces players to real spaceflight concepts including:

  • Orbital mechanics and trajectories
  • Gravity and gravitational influence
  • Rotating reference frames
  • Earth and Moon motion
  • Mass versus thrust trade-offs

By repeatedly launching missions, players develop an intuitive understanding of the challenges faced by real spacecraft navigating through space.

Technology Used

The user interface was initially developed in Python using Pygame, before being reimplemented in C# within the Unity Game Engine.

The simulation itself runs in Unity, with project files shared and managed through GitHub.

Design Considerations

Throughout development, our team focused on the following questions:

  • Will the simulation be realistic?
  • Will the game be engaging and rewarding?
  • Will players learn about trajectories and orbital mechanics?
  • How can we simplify the project while maintaining educational value?
