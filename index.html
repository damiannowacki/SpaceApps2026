Our team is taking on the Space Mission Design Game challenge. We aim to design an engaging game in which the player pilots a spacecraft towards an astronomical body and attempts a successful landing.

Mission Objective:
Target, navigate, and land your spacecraft safely on a chosen astronomical body, balancing skill, accuracy, and mission planning to achieve success.

Real-world considerations

Approaches

  • Direct Ascent
  • Earth-orbit rendezvous
  • Lunar-orbit rendezvous

Gravity

1 kg is 9.8 N on Earth and 1.6 N on the Moon.

Frames of Reference

  • Earth is rotating on its axis
  • Earth is orbiting the Sun
  • The Moon is orbiting the Earth

Physics

Force, speed, energy, work, momentum, thrust, acceleration

Game model

Simplifications

  • Spacecraft leaves earth with a chosen velocity
  • Acceleration of the spacecraft calculated using F=ma, and small delta times
  • Achieving lunar orbit is a success

Gamification

  • Levels: to the ISS, to the moon, to mars
  • Gain SpaceCoin and buy rocket upgrades
