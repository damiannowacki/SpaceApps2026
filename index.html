🚀 Space Apps Challenge 2026

Venue: Met Office, Exeter
Dates: 14th–15th November 2026

Our team is taking on the Space Mission Design Game challenge. We aim to design an engaging game in which the player pilots a spacecraft towards an astronomical body and attempts a successful landing.

Mission Objective:
Target, navigate, and land your spacecraft safely on a chosen astronomical body, balancing skill, accuracy, and mission planning to achieve success.
